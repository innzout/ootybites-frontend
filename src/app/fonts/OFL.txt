  PlusJakartaSans-Variable.woff2   Plus Jakarta Sans — Tokotype
  BricolageGrotesque-Variable.woff2  Bricolage Grotesque — Mathieu Triay

Each is the latin subset of the upstream variable font, as served by Google
Fonts. Self-hosted so the production build has no network dependency and the
customer's browser makes no third-party request for fonts.

To refresh, take the `latin` @font-face src from:
  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@200..800
  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bricolage+Grotesque:opsz,wdth,wght@12..96,75..100,200..800
(request with a modern browser User-Agent, or Google returns ttf instead of woff2)
